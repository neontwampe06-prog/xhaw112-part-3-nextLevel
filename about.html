OUR STORY
Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena was created from Jason Naidoo's vision to build a world-class destination for gamers in Johannesburg and beyond. It began as a fun, competitive space and grew into a community venue for players of every age and skill level.

Why Next Level?

MODERN FACILITIES Premium arena designed for comfort, focus, and performance.
COMPETITIVE ESPORTS Ladders, tournaments, and events for every level of competitor.
INCLUSIVE FOR ALL AGES A welcoming space for gamers of all ages and backgrounds.
HIGH-END TECH Top-tier PCs, consoles, peripherals, and low-latency networks.
